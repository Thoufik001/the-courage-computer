# Original generated assets

Created with OpenAI image generation for this diary, October 8, 2026.

- `quill.png`: transparent photographic goose-feather quill with worn ivory/tawny barbs, exposed shaft and ink-stained cut nib. Prompt requested a complete isolated feather, warm candle illumination, fine translucent edges, portrait composition and no other objects or text.
- `textures/parchment.png`: flat photographic handmade writing-paper texture, warm cream ochre, fine cotton fibers and tiny flecks. Prompt requested uniform lighting, no writing, borders, objects, vignette or dramatic stains.

Original generated PNGs were copied unchanged. The original quill image now textures one curved 3D surface. Its photographed shaft and nib remain part of the single feather, with no duplicate geometry. Both assets may be replaced at the same paths.

- `textures/diary-cover.png`: reference-guided flat albedo cover. Prompt requested dark olive charcoal leather, ornate patinated brass protectors at top-left and bottom-left, the exact lower nameplate “TOM MARVOLO RIDDLE,” and a small dark brass Hogwarts crest, with even lighting and no environment. Generated from the two user-provided diary references and copied unchanged.
- `textures/writing-paper.png`: original flat scan of thin aged laid writing paper. Generated to replace the coarse fiber texture following the user's review. Fine horizontal laid lines, pale ivory, subtle edge aging, no writing or objects.
- `textures/inside-cover.png`: original photorealistic dark leather inside lining with stitched border and worn edges. Preserved as an unused source asset after the inside cover was changed to use the book's own material.

The cover JPEG is used at runtime. `textures/writing-paper-albedo.jpg` is a light-neutralized derivative of the original paper image; the 3D page uses it so the candle and scene lights determine the visible brightness. The original PNGs remain here as editable source assets.
